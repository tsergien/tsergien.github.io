Quick answers

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. It's yours, and it's never sold. Deleting the app deletes it. See the privacy policy.

Can I stop sharing barcode foods? Of course. Switch it off in the app's settings, anytime.

A barcode wasn't found. No problem. Snap the nutrition label instead, or type the food in by hand.

The amounts look wrong. You can always change the grams before saving, and fix any food after it's logged. Nothing is final.

My weight jumped overnight. Did I do something wrong? Not at all. Water, salt and sleep move everyone's weight a little every day. That's why the app shows your weekly trend.

Is this medical advice? No. The app shows educational information from public health sources. For medical questions, please talk to your doctor.
